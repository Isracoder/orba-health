# Orba 🪐

_Enabling menstrual health tracking while doing good_

An offline-first, private, and ethical holistic menstrual tracking app aiming to provide access to secure means of improving and keeping track of menstrual health, while also making it easy for users to present data to their health provider, as well as support female health initiatives. 

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

[Orba](https://orbahealth.org/).



---

## ✨ App Key Features

-   **Private access**: No account needed ! Open the app and track your period
-   **Detailed Logging**: Log and track 24+ symptoms across the month, 
-   **Advanced Insights**: View your cycle and symptom data as visual graphs to see hidden patterns, and share history with your doctor as a PDF
-   **Hormonal Syncing**: Learn about your cycle phases, and build a detailed log of what works for you to customize your advice further. 
-   **Data continuity**: Import/Export your Orba data whenever ! Import cycle history from Flo, Clue, or Apple health as well to maintain continuity
-   **Educational content**: Browse through curated educational articles on the menstrual cycle, common disorders, and take personal notes on any article
-   **Orba AI chat**: Communicate directly with a private AI assistant to ask your private questions or ask for help explaining your data. No names needed, no training on your data.
